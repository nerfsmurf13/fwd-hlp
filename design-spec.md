I actually like their existing palette quite a bit. The problem isn't the colors — it would be how they're organized and applied.

Color hierarchy

The formula should essentially be:

70% white / 20% navy-blue / 8% orange / 2% everything else

Orange should feel valuable because you don't see it everywhere.

For example:

Primary CTA

Orange background, white text.

Secondary CTA

FIND A PLUMBER →

White background, blue border/text.

That mirrors their existing button DNA while making it much cleaner.

I wouldn't make it overly rounded because the mascot has a more traditional, rugged visual character. Big bubbly SaaS cards would clash with him.

The brand direction I'd pitch

I'd describe the visual system as:

Modern Texas Trade Heritage

The white + navy + blue establishes credibility.

The orange creates action and energy.

The vintage plumber gives the organization history/personality.

And the modern typography/UI tells visitors:

"This isn't an old plumber website. This is an organization representing the plumbing profession."

That would let you modernize the hell out of the current site without making the client feel like you threw their existing identity away, which is probably exactly the balance you'd want with this one.
