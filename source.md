### Let's take a look at when modern plumbing practices were relatively new and licensing was not regulated on a state level.

Although the first state legislation to regulate plumbing was passed in 1897, the responsibility of administering standards was delegated to individual municipal governments. As a result, standards and licensing varied significantly depending on the city. A plumber had to obtain a different license in every city that he worked in.

While that might not seem like the most efficient idea, remember... This is the time frame that indoor plumbing became standard in the typical home and we were learning lessons about the importance of proper installations... the hard way.

---

### New London School Explosion

On March 18th, 1937, a great tragedy unfolded in the small East Texas town of New London. On that fateful day, a gas leak under a school building resulted in an explosion that shattered the quiet spring afternoon.

The School Board had decided to cancel their natural gas contract and tap into a "residual gas line" in an effort to save money. What was the result of this decision?

Tragically, hundreds of children and teachers lost their lives in a single event in one day.

The gas piping had been installed by a janitor and a bus driver... A radiator salesman inspected the work.

While plumbing licenses did not become mandatory on a state level until 1947, which led to the formation of the **TEXAS STATE BOARD OF PLUMBING EXAMINERS**, cities began taking plumbing more seriously immediately following the New London tragedy.

## License Laws

Since the 1890's laws have been passed establishing standards and regulations for the Plumbing Industry.

It is important that during both the construction and maintenance of plumbing systems, that the individuals engaged in the work be qualified by training and experience.

It is also important that contractors and service companies comply with the license laws for the financial protection of the homeowner.

Even a company that operates legally under a master plumber, must send out an individual with the proper license and or endorsement to do the work.

The official publication of the Laws that regulate the Plumbing industry can be found on the following link:
* [Occupation Code: Chapter 1301](https://statutes.capitol.texas.gov/?tab=1&code=OC&chapter=OC.1301&artSec=)

You can download an easy-to-read reference version, in pdf format below:
* [Plumbing License Law - reference version](https://tsbpe.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/documents/TSBPE_PlumbingLicenseLaw(PlainView)_Sept2025.pdf)

---

## Board Rules

The Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners adopts rules to interpret, implement and enforce the State Plumbing License Laws.

The Board holds meetings to discuss the direction of the agency. Board meetings are open to the public, anyone can join.

Before a Board rule can be changed, the **Proposed Rule Change** must be presented at a Board meeting and posted for the public to review.

At a subsequent Board meeting, a vote can be held after giving everyone, including the public, a chance to make their comment.

The official publication of the Plumbing Board Rules can be found on the following link:
* [Home - Rules & Meetings](https://texas-sos.appianportalsgov.com/rules-and-meetings?interface=VIEW_TAC&part=17&title=22)

You can download an easy-to-read reference version, in pdf format below:
* [Administrative Rules - January 2025](https://tsbpe.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/documents/TSBPE_AdministrativeRules_March2026.pdf)

## Frequently Asked Questions

*Common Questions*

Understanding state regulations and consumer rights is essential for consumers and plumbing professionals. Below are answers to the most common inquiries regarding licensing and industry standards.

### What are the requirements for a Responsible Master Plumber license?
Master licensure typically requires a minimum of six years of plumbing experience including; documented work history and a series of comprehensive written and hands on exams based upon the design and installation of plumbing systems.

A Master Plumber must file a certificate of insurance with the Plumbing Board in order to obtain a Responsible Master Plumber license. Only an RMP can own or operate a bona fide plumbing company.

### How do I verify a plumber's credentials online?
The Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners provides a public database where you can search by license number or name. We recommend checking the TSBPE website for the most current and verified records.

* [Select Search Type](https://vo.licensing.hpc.texas.gov/datamart/selSearchType.do)

### What consumer rights do I have if a plumber fails to comply with code?
You have the right to a safe and compliant home. If a job is not completed to code, you can file a complaint with your local building department or the TSBPE. If you file a formal complaint with the Plumbing Board, they will investigate.

* [File a Complaint – Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners](https://tsbpe.texas.gov/file-a-complaint/)

### Are there specific safety standards for residential plumbing?
Yes, all jobsites must comply with OSHA safety standards.
